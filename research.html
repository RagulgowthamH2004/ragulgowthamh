Published work

Each has its own page with methods, findings and full citation. Where a DOI exists, the page links to it.

HydrologyMadras Agricultural Journal · 2026·Peer-reviewed

Evaluating Hargreaves–Samani and Valiantzas solar radiation models for FAO-56 reference evapotranspiration

Twenty-five years of NASA POWER data against measured ET₀ at Salem. Hargreaves–Samani reproduced the magnitude almost exactly (5.79 against 5.78 mm day⁻¹); Valiantzas gave the stronger agreement at R² = 0.982. First and corresponding author.
ClimateInt. J. Environment & Climate Change · 2025·Peer-reviewed

Monsoonal dynamics and rainfall extremes in Salem: insights from a climate variability perspective

Reading the district rainfall record as a variable system rather than a fixed climatology — and what that changes about where the extremes sit relative to the planning mean.
ClimateAgriculture & Food e-Newsletter · 2026·Technical article

ENSO–IOD coupling and monsoon response in the Indian Ocean basin, 2014–2025

Phase evolution read through Niño-3.4, the Dipole Mode Index and satellite sea surface temperatures, set against monsoon rainfall departures across eleven years.
ResearchB.Tech. research project·Featured project

Electrostimulation and the growth of Amaranthus in controlled environment agriculture

Defining the treatment by current density rather than voltage turned out to be the whole methodological argument. Root biomass rose 85 per cent — far more than shoot biomass, which points at a local mechanism.
