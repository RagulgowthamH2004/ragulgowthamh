Peer-reviewed research

  1. 2026

    Evaluation of Hargreaves–Samani and Valiantzas solar radiation models for FAO-56 reference evapotranspiration estimation in Salem (Valapadi), Tamil Nadu First author

    Ragul Gowtham, H.,* Anto Rashwin, A., Ramachandran, J., & Rajkishore, S. K. · Madras Agricultural Journal 113(4–6), 198–210 · doi.org/10.29321/MAJ.10.261330

    Designed the study and evaluated both models against 25 years of NASA POWER data. Hargreaves–Samani reproduced measured ET0 almost exactly (5.79 vs 5.78 mm day⁻¹); Valiantzas gave the stronger overall agreement. Useful where local radiation instrumentation doesn't exist — which is most of Tamil Nadu.

  2. 2025

    Monsoonal dynamics and rainfall extremes in Salem, Tamil Nadu: insights from a climate variability perspective First author

    Ragul Gowtham, H., & Ramachandran, J. · International Journal of Environment and Climate Change 15(7) · doi.org/10.9734/ijecc/2025/v15i74911

    Salem sits in the rain shadow of the Western Ghats and draws on both the southwest and northeast monsoons, which makes its rainfall record unusually sensitive to shifts in either system. The paper reads that record through a climate-variability lens, looking at how seasonal totals and rainfall extremes have behaved over the observed period and what that implies for water planning in the district.

Featured project

Electrostimulation as a growth intervention for Amaranthus spp. in controlled-environment agriculture

Student researcher · Agricultural Engineering College & Research Institute, TNAU, Coimbatore

Can a low-intensity direct current through the root zone measurably change how a crop grows? It sits at the intersection of electrical engineering, plant physiology and controlled-environment agriculture, and the literature is thin on reproducible dosing.

  • Designed and instrumented the rig — silver electrodes, regulated DC supply, milliammeter and multimeter — with treatment scheduled across crop-growth stages and matched control and treated groups.
  • Defined the stimulus by current density (A m⁻²) rather than raw voltage or current, so the electrical dose is reproducible and comparable across replicates. This is the part most published work skips.
  • Measured shoot and root length and biomass, Leaf Area Index, and chlorophyll a, b and total, then tested treatment effects statistically.
